Footnotes, images, HTMLFootnotes gather at the message's end. Local images draw as thumbnails where the agent's files are on this device; web images are never fetched. HTML is never shown raw.

The parser now covers what agents write. Raw pipes are gone,1 and bare links like https://github.com/bailycase/shepherd stay tappable.2

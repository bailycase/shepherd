NWSidebarSection(.designs)Destinations never move. Needs you only appears while something waits on you. Designs have their own section, not a place in Recents; a remote host shows as a tag on the row.
Thread list sectionsThe list under the destinations is one piece, SidebarThreads, built from SidebarSectionHeader and SidebarRow. Every screen imports it, so a change here reaches all of them. Order is most urgent first: Needs you (blocked on you), Working, Done (finished and unseen, with Mark all seen), Recents, then Designs. A section with nothing in it is not drawn. Clicking a thread in Done keeps it there while you read it; it moves to Recents when you open another thread.

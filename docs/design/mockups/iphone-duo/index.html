Shepherd on iPhone Duo

proposal mock · not built · rev 2 (HIG geometry)

The proposal is to stop fighting the system and let iPhone Duo's own chrome do the work. Closed, the outer display is a thumb-sized inbox with the system's trailing control column. Open, the same app becomes a split view. Half open, the system splits that view at the fold, and Shepherd adds only one thing: an ArrangementView that puts the composer under the fold. Every device is drawn at 1 pt = 1 px.

Duo facts

Outer display 466 × 678 pt (1398 × 2034 px). Wider and shorter than any other iPhone. Corners are 8 pt on the hinge (left) side and 59 pt on the right. The Dynamic Island is in the top-right corner.
simulator profile · HIG device layout
Inner display 951 × 669 pt landscape, or 669 × 951 pt in the laptop pose. 55 pt corners. Each half is about 462 × 669, roughly the size of the outer display.
simulator profile
Fold ≈ 27 pt through the centre (x or y ≈ 475). It's a reserved region only while half open. Fully open there's no fold in the UI. The inner camera is hidden unless active.
HIG · Preparing your app for iPhone Duo
Vertical controls. On the outer display, and on the inner display in landscape, the nav bar, toolbar and tab bar stack in a trailing column (island, time over Wi-Fi, toolbar, gap, tab bar). Items there are icon-only. The inner display in portrait keeps horizontal bars.
HIG · Vertical controls
Split view. The leading pane keeps horizontal controls at the top (Mail). The detail's controls are vertical on the trailing edge. Fully open, the sidebar is narrower than the detail. Half open, the columns become equal halves at the fold (Notes).
HIG · Split views · mail-full, notes-sidebar-folded
One layout, not one per pose. Start from compact width on the outer display and regular width on the inner one. Move only what has to clear the fold, and keep state the same across displays. ArrangementView overlay: the primary view floats over the secondary when open or closed, and goes below or after the fold when half open. Overflow runs bottom to top.
HIG · Layout · ArrangementView (SwiftUI)
Needs you (lantern, haloed) Running (blue, open ring) Done (green, solid) Each dot sits under a section heading or beside a status word. HIG ▸ marks the Apple rule a decision follows.

P1 · closed · Home

Home, inbox first, five tabs

Home is only the inbox. Designs, Projects and Automations are tabs beside Home and Settings, in the system column.

  • HIG ▸ Tab bars are for navigation Designs, Projects and Automations are top-level destinations, so they're tabs, not rows on Home. Five tabs is the most that fit before iOS adds a More tab, which the HIG says to avoid.
  • HIG ▸ Vertical controls The column follows Apple's order: island, time over Wi-Fi, the toolbar (Compose), a gap, then the five-tab capsule. Search sits under Compose as a toolbar item; it opens the existing search palette.
  • HIG ▸ Badges for critical information The Home tab carries the Needs you count. iOS draws badges red by default; this shows lantern (AgentState.attention) to match the dot.
  • HIG ▸ Don't hide tabs Designs is a tab even when no host serves designs. Its screen says why, rather than the tab disappearing as the Home row does today.
  • HIG ▸ Overflow runs bottom to top Compose is the top toolbar item, so it's the last to overflow. It's the screen's one lantern control.
  • HIG ▸ Icon-only when vertical square.and.pencil, then house, pencil.tip, square.stack.3d.up, bolt, gearshape (the symbols the Home rows use today). The labels move to VoiceOver.
  • HIG ▸ Compact width is the base The 390 pt content column is the familiar iPhone width, so the row layout is unchanged.

What changed vs today

  • The four nav rows (about 40% of 678 pt) are gone. Designs, Projects and Automations are tabs. More's contents (hosts, design systems, extensions) move into Settings.
  • Not a Duo-only change: it's the same TabView in PhoneShell, so a regular iPhone gets the same five tabs along the bottom.
  • No macbook badge on rows and no "connected" line under the title while there's one host and it's online. A host shows up only when something needs attention: the existing offline notice card at the top of the list. No chevrons either.
  • Working rows show a verb ("Running python") instead of the raw command.
  • Search moves up beside New thread, so both actions sit together at the top of the column and the list starts right under the title. Compose has the highest visibility priority, then Search; with five tabs the column still has about 150 pt to spare.

There's no room for a pane closed, so the same five tabs are rows in the thread's ⋯ menu. Each opens as its own screen, pushed over the thread, with Back at the top of the column.

  • HIG ▸ Same functions on every display Everything the open display's pane has is one tap away here, with the same names, icons and status (totals, "1 running", the port).

What changed vs today
